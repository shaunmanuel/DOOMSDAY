Why this order

  • Tier 1 first. Aptitude, Maths, Discrete Maths and Digital Logic are treated as the highest-priority marks engine.
  • DS before Algorithms. Data structures provide the raw material for algorithmic problem solving.
  • TOC after the core engines. Keep Theory of Computation in Tier 2 instead of opening it alongside lower-priority subjects.
  • Tier 3 is the reliable-scoring block. DBMS, OS, Networks and COA are handled only after the first two tiers are locked.
  • Compiler is managed last. It belongs to Tier 4, together with low-yield corners that should not steal time from higher-priority subjects.

The rules

  1. Rule 1 · Tier gate

    Never start a lower tier while the current tier is unfinished.

  2. Rule 2 · Subject gate

    Within each tier, follow the displayed subject sequence from left to right. Finish the current subject before opening the next.

  3. Rule 3 · Aptitude daily

    Aptitude runs every day as a parallel marks engine; it does not count as a reason to skip the tier sequence.

  4. Rule 4 · Watch, then prove

    Watch the day's recording, make notes, then solve PYQs on exactly that topic.

  5. Rule 5 · Mocks stay fixed

    Mocks and tests remain on their dates even when a tier is in progress. They are assessment days, not permission to open a later subject.

  6. Rule 6 · New content ends

    Finish Tier 4 by Thu 21 Jan. From Fri 22 Jan, focus on revision, error correction and mocks.

  7. Rule 7 · Track every miss

    Every wrong answer gets one label (C, S, T or G) and one concrete fix in the Doom Log.

Where it is tight

  • December (OS). OS has the biggest lecture load (about 35–50 h at 2×) and shares December with DS + C and Algorithms. Use Saturdays fully and do not skip Night Watch.

  • Discrete Maths. No classes are announced. If none appear by the 1 Jan lane start, the lane uses NPTEL or a standard text, which is slower than watching.
  • No spare room. A missed weekend lane day cannot be repaid from a mock day. Repay from Sunday revision halves or the 22 Jan patch day.
